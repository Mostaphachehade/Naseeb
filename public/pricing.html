What things cost

Three different people can pay us nothing, nothing, and something. This page says which is which, and it only lists things that actually exist.

This page used to advertise three hosting plans — a free pilot, AED 250 for three listings, and AED 900 a month for unlimited hosting with featured placement and a verified badge. None of them was ever built. There was no checkout, no listing limit, no featured placement and no entitlement behind any of it, and hosting was open to anyone with a verified email regardless of which plan they picked. They have been removed rather than repriced, and nothing has been invented to replace them.

Questions

Do entrants ever pay anything?

No. Entry is always free, for every giveaway, for every entrant, no exceptions. There is no field for an entry price anywhere in the code.

Why is hosting closed?

Because a giveaway published here is a public promise to strangers that a real prize will reach a real winner, and we would rather know who is making that promise. Until this platform has been reviewed and is running at a size we can stand behind, host access is granted one account at a time.

What happens after I apply?

Your account moves to "waiting for review" and nothing else changes — you cannot publish a giveaway while an application is open. An administrator approves or refuses it, with a recorded reason. You will see the outcome on your dashboard.

Will hosting stay free?

We do not know, and we are not going to state a future price we have not decided. What we can say is what is true today: there is no paid hosting tier, no checkout for one, and nothing in the application flow charges anyone.

Could my host access be taken away?

Yes. An administrator can suspend hosting access, with a recorded reason. Suspension stops you publishing new giveaways or drawing winners; it does not delete your existing giveaways, entries or records, and any open prize claim moves to administrator handling so a winner is not left waiting.
