Entry integrity

The platform enforces one entry per verified account per giveaway. It cannot tell whether two verified accounts are two people, and it does not collect identity documents to find out — so anything beyond that constraint is a judgement you make, with a reason, on the record. Disqualifying an entry never deletes it.

Suspended-host claims

Unfinished claims whose host we suspended. You can take the steps the host would have taken — preparing delivery, then marking it shipped or arranged. You cannot claim for the winner and you cannot confirm receipt for them: only the winner can say a prize arrived.

Privacy requests

Access, correction, deletion and objection requests from account holders. Every decision needs notes, and the sentence the requester reads comes from a fixed list — your notes are never shown to them. Closing a deletion request does not delete anything.

Loading…

Email change delivery

Verification links to a new address, and the warning to the old one after a change completes. Failed deliveries are listed first. Retrying is deliberate and needs a reason — it is recorded against the notification.

Loading…

Prize claims needing review

Disputes raised by a winner or host, and claims whose window expired. An expired claim is never redrawn or cancelled automatically — someone won, and it takes a person to decide what happens next.

Drawn giveaways with no claim

Giveaways drawn before this workflow existed, or whose claim was never created. Issuing a claim emails the winner the draw already chose — it never picks a different one.
