1. What Naseeb is, and what it is not

Naseeb is a curated premium-giveaway platform. Sponsors submit prizes; Naseeb reviews and approves every prize before it is published. We provide the listing, the entry record, the random draw, and the record of what happened afterwards.

Naseeb will coordinate fulfilment for approved campaigns and will be the winner's point of contact. The intended model is that we manage communication with the winner and coordinate delivery, booking or redemption. That workflow is not yet operating, and no campaign may run until it is — see the open questions at the end of this draft. Who physically holds a prize differs per campaign and is stated on each listing: Naseeb may hold it; the sponsor may hold it under a commitment to Naseeb; or a provider — a hotel, restaurant or venue — may fulfil it directly while Naseeb coordinates the booking. We do not claim to physically possess or personally deliver every prize.

Where the sponsor supplies or funds the prize, that commitment rests on an agreement between Naseeb and the sponsor. That agreement is not yet in place — it is a prerequisite before this platform operates publicly, and until it exists these words are a description of intent rather than an enforceable guarantee. Whether Naseeb would fund an equivalent replacement if a sponsor failed to perform has not been decided, and nothing here should be read as promising one.

A published campaign cannot have its prize withdrawn by the sponsor. It is cancelled only where fulfilment becomes genuinely impossible, or where continuing would be unlawful, fraudulent, unsafe or otherwise prohibited. If that happens, every entrant is told, the campaign and every entry are preserved, and no draw is pretended.

Nothing on this site is legal advice. We make no representation that using Naseeb satisfies any licensing, permit, advertising, consumer-protection or data-protection requirement that may apply to you or to a campaign you run.

2. Membership, entering, and how a campaign closes

Entering a giveaway requires an active, verified Naseeb membership. A membership has no predetermined expiry date: it remains active until it is closed at your request, suspended or terminated under these rules, or closed where legally, operationally or technically required. Signing out, or a browser session expiring, does not end a membership — expired sessions are removed as routine housekeeping and your account is untouched.

Every published campaign runs for exactly one calendar month from publication and stops accepting entries at whichever comes first: the 100th eligible entry, or the one-month deadline. The deadline is never extended. If the deadline arrives with fewer than 100 eligible entries, the draw still runs, from all eligible entries received before closure. If no eligible entry remains, the campaign closes without a winner and the reason is recorded. Exactly one winner is selected, and a winner is never replaced or redrawn automatically.

If an entry is under review when a campaign closes, entries still close on time — the review does not extend anything — and only the draw waits until the review is resolved.

3. Who pays for what

  • Entrants pay nothing, ever. No entry fee, no ticket, no paid odds, no upgrade. If any part of this site appears to ask an entrant to pay in order to enter or to improve their chances, that is a defect — please report it.
  • Hosts may pay Naseeb for platform and marketing services relating to their own listing. That is a fee for a service provided to the host. It is not a stake, it is not pooled with other payments, and it has no effect on any draw.
  • Advertisers may pay Naseeb for banner placement on the site for a defined period. Advertising is unconnected to any giveaway, entrant or draw.
  • Hosts fund the prizes. Money paid to Naseeb is never used to fund a prize, and prizes are never funded from entrant payments because there are none.

4. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or enter a giveaway. You are responsible for activity under your account and for keeping your password secure.

The platform enforces one entry per verified account per giveaway. It cannot verify that one account corresponds to one person: someone with several verified email addresses could hold several accounts. Holding multiple accounts to obtain extra entries breaches these terms, and entries obtained that way may be voided — but please read "one entry per account" as a description of what the system enforces, not a guarantee about people.

Individual hosts may set their own additional eligibility conditions (for example, residency or age above 18) in their listing. Those conditions are the host's, not ours, and we do not verify them.

5. Hosting a giveaway

If you host a giveaway you confirm that entry is free to everyone with no purchase necessary; that you have disclosed who is funding the prize, which is shown publicly on the listing; that you will honour the result of the random draw; and that you will deliver the prize you described.

You are responsible for ensuring your campaign complies with whatever law applies to you, including any permit or advertising requirement. Naseeb does not check this and does not advise on it.

6. How winners are selected

Once the stated deadline has passed, a winner is selected from all valid entries using a cryptographically secure random number generator, with each entry equally likely. A draw cannot be run before the deadline, and cannot be re-run once completed. The winner's display name and ticket number are shown publicly on the giveaway and on the Winners page.

7. Claiming a prize

A winner is emailed a single-use claim link, which expires after a limited period. To receive a physical prize you will need to provide delivery details — a name, phone number and address — and to agree explicitly to those details being shared with that giveaway's host. We record which version of that consent wording you agreed to, and when.

You may decline. If you do, no delivery details are stored and the prize is not delivered.

If a claim link expires unused, the claim is referred to an administrator for review. We do not automatically redraw the prize, reassign it, or cancel it because a claim window closed. An administrator may reissue the link to the same winner, which invalidates any previous link.

8. Delivery and confirmation

The host marks a prize as being prepared and then as sent or arranged. Only the winner can confirm that a prize was received — a host cannot close a delivery on the winner's behalf.

Delivery details you provide are encrypted and are erased a limited time after a delivery is confirmed or a dispute is closed. That retention period is provisional pending advice from qualified UAE counsel — see our Privacy Policy for the current periods.

Either the winner or the host may raise a dispute, giving a short reason. Only an administrator can resolve a dispute, and must record a reason for the decision. Every step is timestamped and attributed. An administrator's decision is an operational one about the record on this platform; it does not determine anyone's legal rights and does not prevent either party pursuing a remedy elsewhere.

9. Cancellation and suspension

A host may cancel a giveaway before a winner is drawn; entrants are not entitled to compensation for a cancelled giveaway, since nothing was paid to enter. After a draw, a giveaway cannot be cancelled to avoid delivering the prize.

We may suspend or remove an account or listing that breaches these terms — including multi-accounting, misrepresenting who funds a prize, listings that are not genuinely free to enter, or fraudulent host applications. Where we can, we will say why.

Advertising is sold for a defined date range. If we fail to run a placement that was paid for, we will refund it or reschedule it, at the advertiser's choice. Refunds are otherwise at our discretion, and a placement that has already run is not refundable. If a payment is refunded or charged back, the placement stops running.

Host plan pricing and any refund terms attached to it are to be confirmed — see the open questions below. Do not treat the pricing page as a binding offer until that is resolved.
