Submit a prize

Naseeb features carefully selected premium prizes intended to create genuine excitement, happiness, and memorable experiences. Every prize is reviewed and approved by Naseeb before publication. Entry is free, always — participants never pay anything.

How a campaign runs

  • You submit the prize. Nothing publishes automatically — Naseeb reviews every submission first.
  • Once approved, the campaign is published and runs for exactly 30 days from that moment.
  • It closes at whichever comes first: the 100th eligible entry, or the 30-day deadline.
  • If the deadline arrives with fewer than 100 entries, the draw still runs, from all eligible entries received.
  • The draw runs on its own. Nobody has to press anything, and the deadline is never extended.
  • Entry requires an active, verified Naseeb membership.
  • Naseeb will be the winner-facing contact and will coordinate delivery or fulfilment. If a winner has a problem, they will come to us. This workflow is not yet operating, and no campaign can be published until it is.
  • Once published, a prize cannot be withdrawn. A campaign is cancelled only where fulfilment becomes genuinely impossible or continuing would be unlawful.

Naseeb is under development. It is not a licensed, VAT-registered or commercially operating business, and nothing here has been reviewed by qualified counsel.

Naseeb does not publish promotional merchandise, ordinary food or groceries, samples, discount codes presented as prizes, or used, damaged or unsafe goods. High-end dining experiences yes; an ordinary meal, no.
