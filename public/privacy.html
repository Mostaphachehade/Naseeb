Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026. This policy is being reviewed by UAE legal counsel; some wording may change as a result.

What we collect

When you create an account: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password — it's hashed with bcrypt before it touches the database). When you apply to host: your name or business name, contact email and phone, and any trade license or message you choose to include. When you host or enter a giveaway: the giveaway details you submit, and a record that you entered (so we can enforce one entry per person and run the draw).

How we use it

To run your account (signing in, showing your listings and entries), to run giveaway draws fairly, to follow up on host applications, and to send account-related email — verifying your address, password resets, and (if you're a host) confirmations related to your application. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies and local storage

Your session is kept in your browser's local storage as a signed token, so you stay signed in between visits — clearing your browser's site data signs you out. We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic (which pages get visited, roughly how many people browse or enter giveaways); it sets standard analytics cookies to do this. We don't use it for advertising, and it doesn't affect your account or entries.

What hosts see

If you enter a giveaway, the host can see your name and ticket number. Hosts never see your email address, password, or the details of any other giveaway you've entered.

If you're drawn as the winner, nothing further is shared unless you choose to share it. You'll get a link to claim your prize, and on that page you decide whether to provide delivery details — a name, phone number and address for the courier. Only if you tick the consent box are those details passed to that one host, and only the fields needed to deliver something. We record which version of that consent wording you agreed to and when. If you don't claim, no delivery details exist and the host receives nothing.

Prize delivery

Delivery details you provide are encrypted before they're stored, and can be read only by the host of that giveaway, by you, and by our administrators if you or the host raise a problem that needs looking into.

The host marks when a prize has been sent, but only you can confirm you received it — a host cannot close a delivery on your behalf. Either of you can raise a dispute, which an administrator reviews and resolves with a recorded reason.

Once a delivery is confirmed or a dispute is closed, we keep the encrypted delivery details for a limited period and then erase them. We keep a record that the prize was delivered, and the history of who moved the claim along and when, but not your address or phone number. The current retention period is provisional and is one of the things we are asking UAE legal counsel to confirm — we'll update this page when it's settled.

Data retention

We keep account and giveaway data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us — see our About page.

Third parties

We use third-party services to operate Naseeb: a hosted Postgres database to store data, an email provider to send verification, account and prize-claim emails, and (if you upload an image when hosting) an image hosting service to store that file. None of them receive your password, and none of them receives a delivery address or phone number — those are never put in an email, and are only visible to the host after you consent, by signing in.

Your rights

You can review or correct your account details at any time by signing in, and you can request that we delete your account and data. Contact us through the details on our About page for either.

Changes

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
