Who is responsible for your data is not yet published. The legal entity acting as data controller, its registered address, and a contact point for data-protection requests are marked to be confirmed below. We would rather leave them blank than name something we have not verified.

What we collect, and why

Your account: your name, email address, and a hashed password. We never store your password itself — it is hashed with bcrypt before it reaches the database. We use this to sign you in, to show your listings and entries, and to send account email such as verification and password resets.

Confirming you are 18 or older: a record that you ticked a box reading "I confirm that I am 18 years of age or older.", which version of that wording you saw, and when. That is the whole of it. We do not ask for your date of birth, we do not ask for an identity document, and we do not use any service that estimates your age — so this is your declaration, not a check we have carried out, and we say so rather than implying otherwise. It is not shown publicly anywhere. If your account was created before we started asking, we hold no answer for you and will ask before your next entry rather than assume one.

Entering a giveaway: a record that you entered, and your ticket number. This is what makes the draw possible and what stops the same account entering twice.

Changing your email address: the address you are moving to, the one you are moving from, and the one-time link we send to the new address — kept only as a hash, so we cannot read it back. We keep the record of the change afterwards. We also tell your old address that a change was asked for, without putting the link in that message.

Asking us to do something about your data: the type of request, anything you choose to write, its status, and what we decided. Our internal working notes on a request are kept separately and are never shown to you — they can mention other accounts and unproven concerns. What you are shown instead is a plain statement of the outcome chosen from a fixed set of wordings.

Hosting a giveaway: the listing you write, including who you say is funding the prize, which is shown publicly.

Applying to host on a paid plan: your name or business name, contact email and phone, and any trade licence number or message you choose to include.

Claiming a prize: if you win and choose to claim, the delivery details you provide — a name for delivery, a phone number, an address, and any delivery note. Nothing more: we do not ask for identity documents, dates of birth or payment details, and if a browser sends fields we did not ask for, they are discarded rather than stored.

Advertising with us: your business name, contact email, the banner and destination link you supply, and a record of the booking. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

Entry integrity signals: when you enter a giveaway we record, alongside that entry, a one-way keyed hash of a coarsened version of your network address — an IPv4 /24 or an IPv6 /48, never the address itself. The raw address is never written to our database. The hash is keyed with a dedicated secret and with the current retention period, so it cannot be reversed to an address and stops matching across periods. We use it for one purpose: to notice when several accounts created in the last two days enter from the same network, or when entries arrive faster than a person clicking, so that an administrator can look. These are indicators, not conclusions, and nothing about them is automated: no signal disqualifies anyone, blocks an entry or restricts an account. We do not fingerprint your browser or device, do not use identity documents, facial recognition or biometrics, do not consult data brokers or people-search services, and do not track you across other sites.

Entry review and disqualification

The platform enforces one entry per verified account per giveaway. It cannot verify that one account is one person, and it does not collect identity documents to try. If we have reason to think entries are not honest, an administrator — not an algorithm — may place an entry under review or disqualify it, and must record a written reason for doing so. Your entry is never deleted: the record that you entered, when, and under which account is preserved whatever the outcome, and a decision can be reversed with its own recorded reason.

You can see the status of your own entry on the giveaway page and on your dashboard. Where a decision has been made, you are given a short standard explanation of what it means — chosen from a fixed set of approved wordings, not written freely about you. The administrator's own working notes are kept separately for the audit record and are never shown to you, to any host, or in any public page: they can refer to other accounts and to unproven concerns, and neither belongs on your screen. You will also not be shown how anything was detected. If you want to know more about a decision, please get in touch and a person will answer.

What hosts see

If you enter a giveaway, the host sees your display name and ticket number. Hosts never see your email address, your password, or anything about other giveaways you have entered.

If you win, nothing further is shared unless you choose to share it. On the claim page you decide whether to provide delivery details, and only if you explicitly consent are they passed to that one host — and only the fields needed to deliver something. We record which version of the consent wording you agreed to and when. If you do not claim, no delivery details exist and the host receives nothing.

How delivery details are protected

Delivery details are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written to the database, with a separate initialisation vector for each record. They are readable by that giveaway's host, by you, and by our administrators only when a dispute or an expired claim requires someone to intervene. They are never included in an email, never placed in a web address, and never shown in general administrative lists.

The claim link we email you is single-use and expires. It is deliberately carried in the fragment of the web address, which browsers do not send to servers, so it does not appear in our logs.

Prize delivery and disputes

The host records when a prize has been sent; only you can confirm you received it. Either of you can raise a dispute, which an administrator reviews and resolves with a recorded reason. We keep a record of those steps — who did what and when — without keeping your address alongside it.

How long we keep things

All retention periods below are provisional and pending approval by qualified UAE counsel. They reflect our current judgement, not settled legal advice, and may change.

  • Encrypted delivery details: erased on a schedule after a delivery is confirmed or a dispute is closed. The current period is 30 days (provisional).
  • Claim records and their history: retained after the delivery details are erased, so there is a lasting record that a prize was claimed and delivered — without the address.
  • Account, giveaway and entry data: retained while your account is active. A defined period after account closure is to be confirmed.
  • Host applications and advertising enquiries: retained for follow-up; a defined period is to be confirmed.
  • Payment records for advertising: retained as long as required for accounting and tax purposes — the applicable period is to be confirmed.
  • Entry integrity signals (the hashed network indicator described above): deleted after 30 days by default, and the hash stops matching across periods regardless. Provisional.
  • Entry integrity decisions (a review, a disqualification, a reinstatement, the standard explanation given to you, and the administrator's internal notes): retained as an audit record, because a decision about you that nobody can look back at is a decision you cannot contest. This record cannot be edited or deleted once written, including by us, and it holds no address, phone number or network data of its own.
  • Your age confirmation: retained while your account exists, as a status, a version of the wording and a date.
  • Email address changes: the record of a change, including the address you moved from, is kept. A defined period is to be confirmed. The one-time link is only ever held as a hash and stops working once used or once it expires, whichever is first.
  • Requests you make about your data: the request, its history and what we decided are retained, so a decision about you can be looked back at. As with entry integrity decisions, this history cannot be edited or deleted once written, including by us. A defined period is to be confirmed.

Some of the records above hold no name and no address, only an account reference and a date. We do not describe those as anonymous, because they still point at a person if you hold the rest of our database. Whether they may be kept after somebody asks us to delete their data — and whether the references in them would have to be severed — is a question we have written down for counsel rather than answered ourselves.

Who else processes your data

We use the following providers. Where one is optional, it processes nothing unless we have configured it.

  • Render — hosts the application. Processes anything you send to the site in the course of serving requests.
  • Hosted PostgreSQL (currently Neon) — stores account, giveaway, entry, claim and booking data, including encrypted delivery details.
  • Resend — sends verification, password-reset, entry, winner, claim and status emails. Receives your email address and the content of those messages. Delivery addresses and phone numbers are deliberately never put in an email.
  • Stripe — processes advertiser card payments and receives the advertiser's email and payment details directly. Naseeb never sees or stores card details. Entrants never pay anything, so no entrant's payment data exists.
  • Cloudinary — stores images uploaded for giveaway listings and ad banners, when image upload is configured.
  • Google Analytics — measures overall site traffic, when configured. Configured to disable Google Signals and ad personalisation, and not loaded at all on the prize-claim page, your account page, the email-change page, or any administrator page.
  • Sentry — receives application error reports, when configured. Reports are stripped before they are sent: no cookies, no headers, no request contents, no web address beyond the page path, and no account details. Email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, delivery notes, network addresses, one-time links and provider keys are removed automatically wherever they appear, however deeply. Our own log output is not sent at all. This used to depend on us remembering not to write personal data into an error message; it no longer does.

Several of these providers operate outside the UAE, so data reaches other countries. The lawful basis for those transfers, and what safeguards are required, are to be confirmed with counsel.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

Cookies and local storage

Your session is a cookie that scripts on this site cannot read, and it is the only thing that keeps you signed in; clearing your site data or signing out everywhere ends it. Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage. Google Analytics, where configured, sets its own cookies — it is not loaded at all on your account page, the email-change page, a prize claim page, or any administrator page.

Your rights

Signed in, from your account page, you can see what we hold about you, correct your display name, change your email address, download a copy of your data, end every signed-in session, and ask us for access, a correction, a deletion or to object to something. You will be asked for your password again before an export or an email change — being signed in shows that somebody signed in once, not who is at the keyboard now.

What a deletion request is, exactly. It is a request for a person to review, not an erase button. Nothing is deleted when you send one, and nothing is deleted when we close it. Some records cannot simply be removed — an open prize claim, a payment record, or a decision we made about an entry that other entrants relied on — and we will tell you which ones apply to you rather than deleting what is easy and staying quiet about the rest.

Right now we cannot carry out a deletion at all, and we will not pretend otherwise. We have not finished deciding which records can be erased, which can be anonymised, and which we are obliged to keep — so there is nothing that would perform an erasure even if somebody approved one. Because of that, our system will not let anyone mark your deletion request as completed. It stays open, with us, and you will be told plainly that it is waiting rather than being shown a "done" that means nothing. If you would rather we closed it, say so and we will, with a reason on the record.

Being straightforward about the current state: which records could be erased, which could be anonymised, which must be kept and for how long, are questions we have written down rather than guessed at. We have also not published a response deadline, because the timescale that applies under UAE law is to be confirmed with counsel and we are not going to invent one.

Nobody at Naseeb can delete your account from our admin screens either. That control existed, it worked without recording a reason or telling you, and we removed it. An account can be suspended, its sessions can be ended, and a deletion goes through the request process above — where it is weighed against open claims and obligations, and written down.

Your download does not include your delivery address for a prize. That is held encrypted, is erased on a schedule after delivery, and you can see it in the claim itself while it is open or ask for it through an access request. It also excludes anything belonging to somebody else, our internal notes, and the technical signals we use to spot entry abuse.
