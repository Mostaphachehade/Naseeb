The compliant platform for free-entry giveaway marketing.

Giveaways build brands. Most of them can't prove the draw was fair, and paid-entry raffles risk falling under regulated gambling. Naseeb is built free-entry from the ground up — so it's compliant by design, not by policy.

Free entry is not a limitation. It's the moat.

In the UAE, a paid-entry raffle is regulated commercial gaming under the GCGRA (Federal Decree-Law No. 25/2025) — real licensing, real AML/KYC infrastructure, a real minimum fee. A genuine promotional giveaway, free to enter with the prize funded as a marketing cost, sits outside that definition entirely. Naseeb is built to stay in that lane, on purpose, in the code — there's no field for a price anywhere in the entry flow.

Licensed paid-entry operator

Naseeb

Naseeb doesn't offer legal advice, and this isn't it. If you're planning giveaways at a scale where this matters, talk to a lawyer or the GCGRA directly.

Why now

Regulation is catching up

The UAE is formalizing gaming regulation for the first time. That creates market-wide clarity about what's compliant — and Naseeb was built to stay clearly on the right side of that line.

The behavior already exists

Giveaway marketing is already everywhere on social media — unstructured, unverifiable, running on trust alone. Naseeb gives it real infrastructure: a public random draw, disclosed funding, one entry per person.
