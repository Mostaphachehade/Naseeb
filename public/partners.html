Free-entry giveaway marketing, with a record of what happened.

Giveaways build brands. Most of them leave no evidence of how the winner was chosen, or whether the prize ever arrived. Naseeb is a curated premium-giveaway platform, built free-entry from the ground up: entrants never pay anything, and every draw and delivery leaves a record.

Naseeb is not a general freebie, coupon or sample site. Every prize is reviewed and approved by Naseeb before publication, and a submission does not go live until it is.

What Naseeb publishes

Prizes people would genuinely be delighted to win: premium electronics; luxury accommodation and holidays; designer clothing, watches, bags and accessories; high-end dining experiences; premium beauty and wellness packages; concerts, sporting events and exclusive experiences; premium home technology and appliances; vehicles and other major prizes; valuable vouchers from reputable brands; and other lawful, high-quality products or experiences we deliberately approve.

What we decline: cheap promotional merchandise; ordinary food or grocery items; samples; discount-only offers presented as prizes; used, damaged, counterfeit, unsafe or unlawful goods; anything requiring an undisclosed purchase; misleadingly inflated values; unreasonable or undisclosed restrictions; and prizes that depend on expiring stock or a commitment we cannot rely on. There is no minimum price — premium is a judgement about quality, not a threshold.

How a campaign runs

You submit a prize; nothing publishes automatically. Once Naseeb approves it, the campaign is published and runs for exactly one calendar month. It closes at whichever comes first — the 100th eligible entry, or the one-month deadline — and the draw runs on its own, from all eligible entries, with no button for anyone to press and no extension. If fewer than 100 people entered, it still draws.

Naseeb will be the winner-facing contact. The intended model is that we manage communication with the winner and coordinate delivery or fulfilment: electronic prizes as a voucher, code or confirmation; physical prizes by delivery or collection we arrange; stays, dining and experiences fulfilled by the provider with Naseeb coordinating the booking. If a winner has a problem, they will come to us. None of this is running yet: the claim workflow in the code is still host-operated, and resolving that is a prerequisite before any campaign is published. Once a campaign is published the prize cannot be withdrawn — a published campaign is cancelled only where fulfilment becomes genuinely impossible or continuing would be unlawful, and every entrant is told.

The sponsor agreement that makes that commitment contractually binding is not yet in place, and it is a prerequisite before Naseeb operates publicly. Website wording is not a contract, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Free entry is the model, not a marketing angle

Paid-entry raffles and lotteries in the UAE fall under commercial gaming regulation, which the General Commercial Gaming Regulatory Authority (GCGRA) oversees. Naseeb does not operate a paid-entry model: entrants never pay anything, there is no field for an entry price anywhere in the code, and no mechanism to buy better odds.

What that means for any particular campaign is a question for a qualified UAE lawyer, not for us. Free entry is one factor among several, and promotional campaigns can still carry permit, advertising, consumer-protection, prize-fulfilment, trade-licensing and data-protection obligations depending on what is being run, by whom, and at what scale. We do not tell you that using Naseeb removes any of those obligations, and nothing on this site has been reviewed or approved by UAE counsel.

Paid-entry raffle

  • Participants pay for a chance to win
  • Falls within commercial gaming regulation in the UAE
  • Licensing, AML/KYC and supervision obligations follow
  • Not something Naseeb supports or intends to support

What Naseeb does

  • Entry is free — no payment, no paid odds, no entry price in the code
  • Hosts fund and deliver their own prizes as a marketing cost
  • Winners drawn at random after the deadline, with a public record
  • Delivery confirmed by the winner, not asserted by the host
This is not legal advice, and it is not a compliance opinion. Naseeb has not been reviewed or approved by UAE legal counsel, and we make no claim that using it satisfies any licensing, permit, advertising or consumer-protection requirement. Before running a promotional campaign in the UAE — particularly at scale, or on behalf of a client — take advice from a qualified UAE lawyer about your specific campaign, and check directly with the relevant authority where a permit may be required.

Why now

Regulation is catching up

The UAE is formalising gaming regulation, and the rules around promotional campaigns are becoming clearer. We built Naseeb free-entry from the start because that is the model we want to run, and we are seeking legal advice on what it requires of us. We will update these pages as that advice arrives.

The behavior already exists

Giveaway marketing is already everywhere on social media — unstructured, unverifiable, running on trust alone. Naseeb gives it real infrastructure: a public random draw, disclosed funding, and one entry per verified account.
